# The third live eval: Ask, the conversation on every page (2026-11-22)

Every case from `evals/learn/cases.json`, once per model, through the real model door (`node evals/learn/run.ts
--live --model both --md`). Each case's lesson becomes the page the app builds (`lessonAskPage`), and the question
goes through Ask's own prompt and the conversation client, with the app's budget (6,000 tokens, thinking on). The
scorer judges each Markdown answer: it is a real answer with no filler, it links only lessons the pack holds, no prose
paragraph runs past 70 words, and the whole stays under 350 words.

**10 of 10 runs pass.** Qwen (careful): 5 of 5, 4.2–22.9 s. Ornith (fast): 5 of 5, 2.3–10.9 s, about twice as fast
on every case.

**The outcome: Ask runs on the fast model by default** (`src/domain/models.ts`). The rule the fast model broke in the
last eval (a line said in one breath) belonged to the ten-line answer Listen read aloud; the conversation's answers
are read on the page and stream as they come, so speed is what the wait feels like. The Models screen switches Ask to
the careful model in one tap.
